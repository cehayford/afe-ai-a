# House Agent - Alternative Naming Options

**Startup/Product**: African Property SaaS Platform  
**Core Function**: Mobile-first property listing, verification, and escrow platform for East & West Africa  
**Target Audience**: Tenants, landlords, and real estate agents in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Tanzania, and South Africa

## Final Recommendation

**If prioritizing**: **International scale + Trust** → **Sheltr** or **Verified Homes**  
**If prioritizing**: **African authenticity + Community** → **Jamii** or **Home Tribe**  
**If prioritizing**: **Memorable + Fun** → **Key Please** or **Nestful**

**My top pick**: **Sheltr**
- Modern, clean, international
- Passes telephone test
- Strong visual branding potential
- Can verb-ify ("Let's Sheltr")
- Scales globally while staying relevant
- Positive without being generic
