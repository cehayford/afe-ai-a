How to read this plan

Each workstream has its features, what it depends on, its size in engineer-weeks, and an exit test. A workstream starts only when its gate is met. Where a feature touches money or someone's legal position, it goes behind a rule or a switch, like protected payments did. Sizes assume the current two-engineer team; §13 shows how they fit.

Strengthened in this round

Before planning new features, we closed the gaps the build plan's status section listed. All of these are built and tested:

  • Malware scanning: uploaded documents are scanned by ClamAV before they're stored, and uploads are refused if the scanner is down. In staging it runs as a container beside the API.
  • Rate limits: "Check before you pay", scam reports and WhatsApp lookups are limited, so no one can scrape who is on Afe or run up the WhatsApp bill.
  • Device links: each browser gets a random device ID, and public accounts used on the same device are linked in the fraud graph.
  • Receipts: tenants and landlords get a PDF receipt for protected payments.
  • Verifier re-checks: 15% of approved homes get an unannounced re-check by a different verifier. Reversal rates per verifier are shown for the monthly review (build plan §5).
  • A fix: the payment partner's confirmation address was missing from the load balancer, so real partner confirmations would never have arrived.

2 · MoneyLaunch offer and pricing

Afe's charges are free for the first 8 months of Year 1: from W1 (5 Oct 2026) to 5 Jun 2027. The code already does this. The end date is a rule, so ending or extending the offer takes two reviewers, not a deploy.

What the offer costs Afe

No fee revenue until June 2027. Afe pays for every verification visit (Phase 0 measures the cost per visit), plus the gateway's charge on every protected payment made before 5 Jun 2027: the pilot (W24–W27) and the first eight weeks open to everyone (W28–W35). At a 1.95% gateway rate that's about GH₵585 on a GH₵30,000 tenancy: roughly GH₵15,000 for the 25 pilot tenancies alone, plus the same per tenancy after that. Plan the Year 1 budget on this. The launch offer moves the break-even date in the build plan's §14 to after W35.

Everything the public launch needs that isn't a feature

W1–W14 · 14 engineer-weeks
Real providersW1–W8 · 4 wks

  • SMS gateway (Hubtel or mNotify) and WhatsApp templates. Meta approval takes weeks, so submit the templates in W1.

Scheduled jobsW3–W4 · 1 wk
  • These jobs aren't wired into the infrastructure yet. Add EventBridge Scheduler to ECS RunTask for: run-due every 5 min, score-pending and publish-outbox every minute, reconcile at 02:00 and detect-graph at 03:00.
  • Alarm when a job fails, when the scoring queue backs up, and when reconciliation finds any difference.

  • Deletion removes profile, messages and photos. The audit log, the money ledger and verification decisions are kept for the legal retention period, with the person's name replaced by a reference.
  • Privacy notice covering the device cookie, model scoring in shadow, and what reviewers see. Record consent. File the Data Protection Commission registration.
Operations basicsW6–W10 · 3 wks
  • Staff admin in the app, not the command line: add and deactivate reviewers and verifiers, change roles, sign out everywhere.
  • Error tracking and CloudWatch alarms for server errors, slow pages, queue backlog and failed sign-in spikes. External uptime checks. A weekly export of the audit log.
ProofW8–W13 · 3 wks

5 · Workstream BRenter growth

More renters finding and trusting verified homes

W14–W30 · 11 engineer-weeks
  • Saved searches and alerts: "tell me when a verified 2-bedroom in East Legon under GH₵5,000 goes live", by SMS or WhatsApp. This is the renter's main reason to come back. (3 wks)
  • Search and book on WhatsApp: go beyond the number check to searching, getting three matching listings, and booking a viewing, all in the chat. Most renters already live in WhatsApp. (3 wks)
  • Map view and neighbourhood pages: each area's verified price range by bedroom count, from Afe's own listings. These pages are indexable and double as marketing. (2 wks)
  • Viewing reminders and no-shows: reminders the day before and an hour before, a no-show record on both sides, and link previews when a listing is shared on WhatsApp. (1 wk)
  • Twi for the core renter journey: search, listing page, "Check before you pay" and the scam warnings. English stays the default. The translation approach is B17. (2 wks)
Status · 24 Sep 2026Built and tested: saved searches and alerts (inbox always, at most 3 SMS or WhatsApp a day), WhatsApp FIND, ALERT, STOP, VIEW and HELP, day-before and hour-before viewing reminders, neighbourhood pages with rent ranges, link previews when a listing is shared, and a map view that shows approximate areas, never exact addresses. Twi: the catalogue and switch are built but hidden until a translator fills it (B17).
Exit (W30)Enquiry-to-viewing rate measured and rising · 1,000+ active alert subscribers · Twi pages reviewed by native speakers

6 · Workstream CTrust and safety operations

Verification that scales without losing quality

W14–W44 · 13 engineer-weeks
  • Visit planning: a reviewer's map of homes waiting for a visit, grouped into daily routes per verifier. Travel time is most of what a verification costs (build plan §5). (3 wks)
  • Re-verification at 12 months: reminders to landlords 30 days before a verification expires, a queue for renewal visits, and listings paused on expiry. The first software verifications expire from about W57 (Nov 2027), so this must be ready by W52. (2 wks)
  • Appeals (B12): a rejected landlord, a removed listing or an upheld report can be appealed once, to a different reviewer, with a written outcome. (2 wks)
  • Reviewer workload: time from application to decision, queue age, and alerts when a service target is at risk. (1 wk)
  • Fraud investigation view: the account-link graph drawn for one person, with the shared details on each link and the decisions already made. (2 wks)
  • Photo matching against public sites: the build plan's Phase 3 item. Compare our photo hashes with public listing sites where their terms allow, to find the same home listed elsewhere. (2 wks)
  • Label quality: a second reviewer re-decides a 5% sample, so the models are only trained on labels people agree on. (1 wk)
Status · 24 Sep 2026Built and tested: re-verification warnings and pausing of lapsed listings, appeals decided by a different reviewer, reviewer workload against a 3-day target, suggested visit routes, a 5% second check of labels, matching against photo hashes from permitted crawls, and a linked-accounts graph. It also fixed the visit queue, which refilled with verified homes 30 days after their visit. Waiting on: a permitted crawl of public listing sites.
Exit (W44)Median time from application to live ≤ 7 days at 2× W14 volume · re-verification ready before the first expiries at W57 · a model promoted beyond shadow (build plan Phase 3 exit)

8 · Workstream EInsights and metrics

Know every week whether the targets are being hit

W20–W40 · 5 engineer-weeks
  • Targets dashboard: the build plan's §13 targets in one place: verified live listings, time to live, confirmed fraud on verified listings, verifier pass and reversal rates, and payments that skip protection. (2 wks)
  • Cost per verification: visit time and travel logged from the field app, against the verification fee, so the B6 price is set from data. (1 wk)
  • Leakage survey: a short question after a viewing ("did you pay outside Afe?") to measure deals that skip protection honestly. (1 wk)
  • Accra verified rent index: a quarterly public page of verified rents by area and size, for press and search engines. (1 wk)
Status · 24 Sep 2026Built and tested: the targets dashboard, the leakage question after viewings, cost per verification from logged travel time and fares (the hourly cost is a placeholder until Phase 0 measures it), and the public Accra rent index.

10 · Workstream GLandlord and agent tools

The Year 2 subscription business

W53–W78 · Oct 2027 to Mar 2028 · 20 engineer-weeks

This is the brief's "later, tools for landlords". It is the recurring revenue that doesn't depend on each let. It only makes sense once landlords already trust Afe with their tenants' money, so it waits for protected payments to open.

  • Rent schedule and reminders: each tenancy's due dates, with reminders to the tenant and a record of what was paid. (3 wks)
  • Monthly rent collection: after the first advance, collect monthly rent through the payment partner, for landlords who choose it. (5 wks, after B7 covers it)
  • Digital tenancy agreements: a full Act 220 agreement from the terms already agreed, signed electronically by both sides. (3 wks)
  • Tenant records, renewals and maintenance requests: one page per home and tenant, renewal offers within the advance cap, and repair requests with photos. (4 wks)
  • Agent workspace: one agent managing several landlords' homes under their written authorisations. (3 wks)
  • Subscription billing: tiers by number of homes, priced under B19. The first 3 months are free for landlords who joined during the launch offer. (2 wks)
Status · 24 Sep 2026Built and tested: the tenant register (Afe tenancies join it automatically), payment records with PDF receipts, daily rent reminders, repair requests, agreements signed in the app, renewals within the advance cap, an agent view by owner, and plans (placeholder prices until B19, free in the launch offer). Waiting on B7: collecting monthly rent through the partner.
Exit (W78)100 paying landlords or agents · monthly churn under 5% · no drop in verification standards for subscribed homes

11 · Workstream HExpansion

One gate at a time

Third neighbourhood W40 · Greater Accra Year 2 · then Kumasi

This follows the build plan's §17 expansion ladder. Each area opens only when its gate is met. Most of the work is people and data, not code.

  • Area onboarding kit: draw the area boundary, import the manual rent survey as price bands, assign verifiers, and open the area to listings only once all three are in place. (2 wks)
  • Country readiness: rules are already per country. Add per-country currency, language and ID-check adapters (Nigeria's NIN, Kenya's national ID) behind the existing interfaces, when B14 picks the second country. (4 wks, Year 2)
  • Per new area: about 1 engineer-week to set up, plus local verifiers and a 10-home hand-verification run, like Phase 0.
Status · 24 Sep 2026Built and tested: the area onboarding kit. An admin creates an area closed, loads its price survey, and it opens only with a boundary, a survey for 2+ sizes and an active verifier. Country readiness waits on B14.

15 · StandardDefinition of done

Every feature in this plan meets all of these before it ships.

  • Automated tests for its rules, including the ways it can be misused
  • Every decision it makes or supports is written to the audit log
  • A privacy check for any new personal data: what, why, who sees it, how long it's kept
  • WCAG 2.1 AA, and usable on a low-end Android phone on a slow connection
  • Anything touching money or legal limits sits behind a rule or a switch, with two-person approval
  • A runbook entry for what can go wrong, and an alarm if it matters
  • The build plan's status section is updated when it ships
